Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

Aquarium Manager does not collect, upload, share or sell any personal information.

What stays on your phone

Device names, schedules and settings, dosing container levels, the manual dose log and your preferences are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. They're removed when you delete the app.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your own aquarium equipment (lights, dosing pumps, heaters, stirrers) and send it the settings you choose. In the background it keeps those connections so heater monitoring and alerts can continue.

Notifications

Alerts such as "heater too hot" or "container low" are created on your phone. Nothing passes through a server.

No tracking

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs, and the app doesn't use the internet.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
