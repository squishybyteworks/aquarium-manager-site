Support

Help with Aquarium Manager. For anything not covered here, open an issue.

A device doesn't show up when adding it

  • Stand near the device. Bluetooth range is usually a few meters.
  • Each device accepts one Bluetooth connection at a time. Close the vendor app, and any hub or other phone using it, then try again.
  • Check that Bluetooth is on and allowed for the app: Settings → Aquarium Manager → Bluetooth.

A change says "waiting to be sent"

The device is out of range. The change is saved and goes out automatically the next time your phone connects to it.

Heater safety

The overheat cut-off is stored on the heater and rewritten every time the app connects, so it protects the tank even when your phone is away. Live monitoring, the failsafe and alerts work while your phone is connected to the heater.

Dosing containers

Enter a container's volume when you fill it (head → Container → Refilled). The app counts down using the pump's own dose counter, so doses run while your phone is away still count.

Try it without equipment

Settings → Explore the demo. Nothing is sent to real devices, and your own settings are untouched.

Contact
